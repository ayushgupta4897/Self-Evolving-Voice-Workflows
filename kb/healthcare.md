# Brightwater Family Health — Verified Member Cost Manual

Authority: this document is the single source of truth for member cost share,
prior authorisation, and referral policy at Brightwater Family Health. Any
amount or rule not stated here is not clinic policy. Last verified 2026-07-24.

---

## 1. Member Cost Share Per Encounter

Amounts below are what the member pays at the time of the encounter. They are
stated per encounter and do not include coinsurance on separately billed
laboratory or pharmacy items.

| Encounter | In network | Out of network |
|---|---|---|
| Specialist consultation | $47 | $135 |
| Primary clinician consultation | $23 | $88 |
| Behavioural health session, 50 minutes | $31 | $119 |
| Urgent care encounter | $74 | $162 |
| Telehealth consultation | $12 | not covered |
| Annual wellness exam | $0 | $96 |
| Physical therapy session, first six | $38 | $107 |
| Physical therapy session, seventh onward | $54 | $107 |
| Nutrition counselling session | $19 | not covered |
| Routine laboratory panel | $9 | $63 |
| Advanced imaging, magnetic resonance | $186 | $418 |
| Advanced imaging, computed tomography | $151 | $377 |
| Dermatology lesion removal, in office | $92 | $241 |
| Allergy immunotherapy injection | $7 | not covered |
| Durable medical equipment fitting | $58 | $144 |

### 1.1 Cost share rules

- The out of network amount applies whenever the treating clinician is not
  listed in the Brightwater network directory, regardless of where the
  encounter took place.
- A member seen for two distinct concerns on the same calendar day pays one
  cost share, the higher of the two.
- Brightwater does not waive or discount member cost share. There is no
  hardship, senior, student, or first encounter reduction.
- An amount quoted by phone is an estimate. It is binding only up to 10 percent
  above the figure quoted. Anything beyond that requires a fresh estimate
  before the member is billed.
- Brightwater does not accept a competing clinic's stated amount as a basis for
  matching. There is no matching policy.

## 2. Prior Authorisation

- Advanced imaging, both magnetic resonance and computed tomography, requires
  prior authorisation before the encounter is scheduled.
- Behavioural health sessions beyond the twelfth in a plan year require prior
  authorisation.
- Durable medical equipment above $400 in billed charge requires prior
  authorisation.
- Standard prior authorisation is decided within 3 business days of a complete
  submission. Expedited review is decided within 26 hours and is available only
  when the ordering clinician marks the request urgent.
- A granted prior authorisation is valid for 92 days from the date of grant.
- A denied prior authorisation may be appealed once, in writing, within 44 days
  of the denial notice.
- Prior authorisation is never granted retroactively for an encounter that has
  already occurred.

---

## 3. Referrals

- A referral from a primary clinician is required for the in network specialist
  amount to apply. Without a referral on file the member pays the out of
  network amount even if the specialist is in network.
- A referral covers up to 4 encounters with the named specialist.
- A referral expires 92 days after issue.
- Telehealth consultations and urgent care encounters never require a referral.

---

## 4. What the Representative Must Not Do

- Never state an amount that does not appear in section 1. If an encounter type
  is not listed, the correct response is that it requires a written estimate
  from the billing office.
- Never state a network dependent amount without knowing whether the treating
  clinician is in network. If that is unknown, ask before stating an amount.
- Never confirm that a prior authorisation will be granted. Only the review
  team decides.
- Never state a decision turnaround shorter than the ones in section 2.
- Never combine several encounter amounts into a single total without saying
  that the figure is a sum of separate estimates.

---

## 5. When to Escalate to a Human

Escalation is appropriate only in these cases:

- The member is disputing a charge on a statement already issued.
- The member is appealing a denied prior authorisation.
- The member reports a clinical safety concern or an injury that occurred at a
  Brightwater site.
- The member asks whether they qualify for the financial assistance programme.
- The member asks about an encounter type not listed in section 1.

Escalation is NOT appropriate for questions about cost share amounts, referral
rules, prior authorisation timelines, network status, or which encounters are
covered. Those are all answerable from this document and must be answered
directly. Transferring a member who asked an answerable question is a failure,
not a safe default.
