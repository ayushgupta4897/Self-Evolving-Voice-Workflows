# Cardinal Ridge Mutual — Verified Policyholder Manual

Authority: this document is the single source of truth for deductibles,
coverage limits, and claim filing rules at Cardinal Ridge Mutual. Any amount or
rule not stated here is not underwriting policy. Last verified 2026-07-24.

---

## 1. Deductibles By Coverage

The deductible is the amount the policyholder bears before Cardinal Ridge pays
on a covered loss. Amounts are per loss occurrence, not per policy term.

| Coverage | Deductible |
|---|---|
| Collision | $612 |
| Comprehensive | $438 |
| Uninsured motorist property damage | $217 |
| Glass only, standalone | $71 |
| Personal effects endorsement | $126 |
| Custom equipment endorsement | $293 |
| Rental reimbursement | $0 |
| Emergency roadside endorsement | $0 |

### 1.1 Limits and allowances

| Benefit | Amount |
|---|---|
| Rental reimbursement, per day | $43 |
| Rental reimbursement, maximum days | 27 |
| Personal effects endorsement, per occurrence limit | $1,850 |
| Custom equipment endorsement, per occurrence limit | $4,200 |
| Loss of use allowance, per day | $31 |

### 1.2 Deductible rules

- Two coverages triggered by a single occurrence draw one deductible, the
  higher of the two.
- Cardinal Ridge does not waive a deductible under any circumstance, including
  a not at fault determination. Recovery of a deductible from an at fault party
  happens through subrogation after settlement, never at filing.
- A deductible amount stated by phone is an estimate and is binding only up to
  10 percent above the figure stated. Beyond that a written schedule is issued.
- There is no multi policy, loyalty, or safe record deductible reduction.

## 2. Claim Filing Windows

- Collision loss must be filed within 31 days of the date of occurrence.
- Comprehensive loss must be filed within 47 days of the date of occurrence.
- Theft must be reported within 96 hours of discovery, and a law enforcement
  report number is required before the file is opened.
- Uninsured motorist property damage must be filed within 61 days.
- A claim filed after its window is closed without review. There is no
  discretionary extension at the representative level.
- Supporting photographs must be uploaded within 14 days of the file opening.
- Cardinal Ridge does not open a claim file on a verbal estimate alone. A
  written estimate from a licensed assessor is required.

---

## 3. Settlement and Payment

- Covered losses are settled at actual cash value, less the applicable
  deductible. Cardinal Ridge does not write replacement cost settlements on
  personal lines policies.
- Settlement payment is issued within 9 business days of a signed release.
- A total loss determination is made when the assessed loss exceeds 78 percent
  of actual cash value.
- Salvage retention by the policyholder reduces settlement by the salvage bid
  amount plus a $185 handling charge.

---

## 4. What the Representative Must Not Do

- Never state a deductible that does not appear in section 1. If a coverage is
  not listed, the correct response is that the policy schedule must be pulled.
- Never state a deductible without knowing which coverage the loss falls under.
  If that is unknown, ask before stating an amount.
- Never confirm that a loss is covered. Coverage determination belongs to the
  assigned adjuster after review.
- Never promise a settlement figure or a settlement date before a release is
  signed.
- Never state that a filing window can be extended.

---

## 5. When to Escalate to a Human

Escalation is appropriate only in these cases:

- The policyholder disputes a settlement amount already offered.
- The policyholder alleges bad faith handling or requests a formal complaint.
- The policyholder reports bodily injury arising from the occurrence.
- The policyholder asks whether a specific loss will be covered.
- The policyholder asks about a coverage not listed in section 1.

Escalation is NOT appropriate for questions about deductible amounts, filing
windows, limits, allowances, or settlement timelines. Those are all answerable
from this document and must be answered directly. Transferring a policyholder
who asked an answerable question is a failure, not a safe default.
