scheduling at Meridian Auto Service. Any figure or rule not stated here is not
company policy. Last verified 2026-07-24.

### 1.1 Pricing rules

- The check engine light diagnostic fee of $140 is waived in full if the
  customer authorises the resulting repair at Meridian.
- The pre-purchase inspection fee of $195 is never waived.
- A quote given over the phone is an estimate. It is binding only up to 10%
  above the quoted figure. Anything beyond 10% requires a new customer
  authorisation before work proceeds.
- Meridian does not price match. There is no price match policy.
- There is no senior, student, military, or first-time-customer discount.
  The only discount offered is the free tyre rotation bundled with an oil
  change, and the diagnostic fee waiver described above.

## 2. Warranty

- Parts and labour on all brake work: 24 months or 24,000 miles, whichever
  comes first.
- Parts and labour on all other repairs: 12 months or 12,000 miles, whichever
  comes first.
- Batteries: 36 months free replacement, prorated thereafter to 60 months.
- Tyres are not sold or warranted by Meridian.
- Warranty is transferable to a new owner of the same vehicle at no cost.
  The original invoice number is required.
- Warranty claims require the vehicle to be presented at a Meridian location.
  Meridian does not reimburse work performed by third-party shops under any
  circumstances.
- Wear items — wiper blades, filters, bulbs, fluids — carry no warranty.

---

## 3. Appointments and Scheduling

- Appointments can be booked up to 60 days in advance.
- The earliest same-day appointment must be booked before 14:00 local time.
- Oil changes, tyre rotations, inspections, and emissions tests are offered as
  walk-in service. All other work requires an appointment.
- Cancellation requires 4 hours notice. A cancellation inside 4 hours, or a
  no-show, incurs a $40 fee charged to the card on file.
- The $40 late-cancellation fee is waived once per customer per calendar year.
- Meridian does not hold vehicles overnight for diagnostic work unless the
  customer has explicitly authorised an overnight hold at drop-off.
- Loaner vehicles are available only for repairs quoted above $800 and must be
  reserved at the time of booking. There are four loaners per location.
- Meridian does not offer pickup or delivery of customer vehicles.

## 4. Hours and Locations

Meridian Auto Service operates two locations.

**Meridian Downtown** — 1420 Harrison Street
- Monday to Friday: 07:00 to 18:00
- Saturday: 08:00 to 16:00
- Sunday: closed
- Service bays: 8. Alignment rack: yes. EV certified: yes.

**Meridian Northgate** — 887 Larkin Avenue
- Monday to Friday: 07:30 to 17:30
- Saturday: closed
- Sunday: closed
- Service bays: 5. Alignment rack: no. EV certified: no.

Rules that follow from the above:
- All wheel alignments are performed at Downtown only.
- All EV work, including EV brake service and EV battery diagnostics, is
  performed at Downtown only.
- Northgate is closed on Saturdays. Saturday work is Downtown only.
- Both locations are closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day.
  All other public holidays are normal business days.

## 5. Payment

- Accepted: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, debit, and cash.
- Not accepted: personal cheques, wire transfer, cryptocurrency, and all
  third-party buy-now-pay-later services.
- Meridian offers in-house financing on repairs above $1,000 through
  Meridian Credit, subject to approval. The application is completed at the
  service desk and cannot be started or approved over the phone.
- A deposit of 50% is required before ordering any special-order part.
  Special-order deposits are non-refundable once the part has been ordered.
- Vehicles are released only after payment clears in full.

---

## 6. Towing and Roadside

- Meridian does not operate its own tow fleet.
- Meridian will dispatch a third-party tow on the customer's behalf. The tow is
  billed directly to the customer by the tow operator, not by Meridian.
- Towing is credited $75 toward the repair invoice if the resulting repair at
  Meridian exceeds $400.
- Meridian does not provide jump starts, lockout service, or fuel delivery.

---

## 7. What the Agent Must Not Do

These are hard boundaries for any Meridian representative, human or automated.

- Never quote a price that does not appear in section 1. If a service is not
  listed, the correct response is that it requires an in-person estimate.
- Never state a vehicle-class price without knowing the vehicle class. If the
  class is unknown, ask for the year, make, and model before quoting.
- Never promise a completion time for diagnostic work. Diagnostic duration is
  not knowable in advance.
- Never confirm parts availability. Parts availability is not visible to the
  phone agent and must be checked by the service desk.
- Never quote a total that combines multiple services without stating that the
  figure is a sum of individual estimates.
- Never agree to a warranty claim on the phone. Warranty claims require the
  vehicle to be presented.

---

## 8. When to Escalate to a Human

Escalation is appropriate only in these cases:

- The customer requests a warranty claim decision.
- The customer disputes a charge on a past invoice.
- The customer reports a safety incident, injury, or vehicle damage that
  occurred at a Meridian location.
- The customer asks about in-house financing eligibility.
- The customer requests a service not listed in section 1.

Escalation is NOT appropriate for questions about price, hours, location,
policy, warranty duration, appointment availability, or accepted payment
methods. Those are all answerable from this document and must be answered
directly. Transferring a caller who asked an answerable question is a failure,
not a safe default.
